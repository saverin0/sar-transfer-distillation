## What the model cards say beyond the licence line

The rest of each card has no other word about a licence. Both cards point to the paper
"SigLIP 2 - Multilingual Vision-Language Encoders with Improved Semantic Understanding,
Localization, and Dense Features" (arXiv 2502.14786, Tschannen et al., 2025) and say the
model "is pre-trained on the WebLI dataset". Both cards describe the intended use as
zero-shot image classification, image-text retrieval and "as a vision encoder for VLMs (and
other vision tasks)".

## What this means here

- Apache-2.0 allows use, change and sharing under its conditions. It asks for its licence
  text and for marks on changed files. Nothing in it binds a model distilled from the
  weights to the same terms.
- No weight file of the model is copied into this repository. The weights are downloaded
  once from the two repositories at the pinned revisions into the Hub cache.
- This file records what the pages said. It is not legal advice.
- Before anything is published the two pages are read again, and a new dated file is
  written if one of them has changed.
