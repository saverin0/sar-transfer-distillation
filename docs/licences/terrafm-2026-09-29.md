## What this means here

- Apache-2.0 and MIT both allow use, change and sharing under their conditions. Apache 2.0
  asks for its licence text and for marks on changed files. MIT asks for the copyright
  notice and the MIT text in every copy.
  Which of the two holds for TerraFM is not settled by its authors.
- The copied model file in `src/sartransfer/distil/teachers/vendor/terrafm/` carries the
  Apache License 2.0 text. It carries no MIT text, because the authors have published none.
  The reasons are in the NOTICE.md of that folder.
- This file records what the pages said. It is not legal advice.
- Before anything is published the three places are read again, and a new dated file is
  written if one of them has changed.
